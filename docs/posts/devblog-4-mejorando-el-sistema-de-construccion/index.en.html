The current structure of a block is:

The model and, as children, the GridStuds it has.
To save myself a component on each GridStud telling me its dimensions, I put them in its name and then parse it.
Since at most you'll always interact with 1 block (as far as figuring out its studs/using them goes), I think it's a very efficient approach even if it makes that data a bit harder to access.
