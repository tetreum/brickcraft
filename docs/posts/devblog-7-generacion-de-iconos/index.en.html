Since progress on generating the map via chunks is zero, I've decided to move forward on this front instead.
As I mentioned in previous posts, we need a system to generate the icons for every item in the game, so we don't have to do it by hand.

For that we'll use the green screen technique. We'll put 1 brick in the center of the scene against a green background and center the camera on it to take a picture.

We start by setting the camera background to a garish green and removing visual effects like eye adaptation (because otherwise the green will get shadows and won't be the same green).

Next, instead of showing the camera output on screen, we send it to a texture and crop that texture to the size we want. In the resulting texture, to turn the green transparent, we grab the very first pixel (which will be on an edge of the image) and then go through aaaaall the pixels of the texture to find which ones are equal to it and make them transparent:

With this, we're left with the other tricky part: centering the camera on the object so it's always fully visible.
Lego has very long bricks and very small ones, so we can't use the same camera position in every case. We have to calculate the size of the brick, the camera's "field of view" and apply an offset.

It was coming out like crap for me, so I had to look up how to calculate it properly.
